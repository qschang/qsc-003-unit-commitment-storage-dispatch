# District heat pump and thermal store ledger

An energy operator must reconstruct a one-day district-heating schedule from hourly heat demand, ambient temperature and two available heat sources. Implement `/app/dispatch.py` and run it as `python3 /app/dispatch.py INPUT.json OUTPUT.json`.

The UTF-8 input object contains exactly `horizon_hours`, `periods`, `boiler`, `heat_pump`, and `thermal_store`. Each period has `demand_MWh`, `ambient_C`, and `electricity_price`. The boiler describes `max_heat_MW`, `min_heat_MW`, `efficiency`, and `fuel_price`; the heat pump describes `max_heat_MW`, `min_ambient_C`, `cop_base`, and `cop_slope`; the thermal store describes `capacity_MWh`, `initial_MWh`, `max_charge_MW`, `max_discharge_MW`, `charge_efficiency`, `discharge_efficiency`, and `terminal_MWh`.

Produce exactly `schedule` and `summary`. Every schedule record must preserve period order and contain boiler heat, heat-pump heat, storage charge and discharge, stored energy, unmet heat, and operating cost. The heat-pump coefficient of performance is `cop_base + cop_slope * ambient_C` whenever the ambient threshold is met. Heat balance, source limits, storage recursion, terminal inventory, finite-number rules, and nonnegative demand must hold. Unmet heat is allowed only after available sources and storage are used and is charged at public penalty `20000.0` per MWh. Operating cost is fuel heat divided by boiler efficiency times fuel price plus heat-pump electricity (heat divided by COP) times the period electricity price plus the unmet penalty.

Reject duplicate keys, missing or extra fields, booleans in numeric positions, non-finite or negative values, invalid efficiencies, impossible horizons, an unavailable heat pump used below its ambient threshold, and a terminal inventory that cannot be reached. Validate the complete input before publishing. A rejected run must leave the destination absent or byte-for-byte unchanged and must leave no temporary, report, ledger, summary, or checkpoint files. Repeating an accepted run with the same input must produce identical bytes.
